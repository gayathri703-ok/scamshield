Red Flags

Scam Warning Signs

If even one of these patterns appears in a job offer — walk away immediately.

💸

Recruiters Asking for Money

Legitimate companies NEVER charge registration fees, training deposits, or security money. Any payment demand = scam.

📧

Gmail Instead of Company Domain

Real Infosys recruiters use @infosys.com. "infosys.hr@gmail.com" is always fake — zero exceptions.

⚡

Instant Selection Without Interview

"You're hired!" with zero evaluation? Real companies run written tests, technical rounds, and HR interviews.

📱

Telegram / WhatsApp Interviews

No reputable MNC conducts interviews on Telegram or WhatsApp. These are scammer favourites.

💰

Unrealistic Salary Promises

₹80,000/month for zero-experience fresher? Scammers exploit greed. Research market rates first.

🔗

Lookalike / Suspicious URLs

"wipro-careers.org" or "amazon-india-jobs.net" are fake. Verify the exact official domain letter by letter.

Stay Informed

Cyber Awareness Hub

Understanding attack types is your first and strongest line of defence.

🎣

Phishing Attacks

HIGH RISK — Most Common

Fraudulent emails or cloned websites mimicking trusted brands to steal credentials and banking info.

Check the actual sender domain — not just the display name
Hover over links before clicking to verify the real URL
Never enter login details from a link inside an email
📋

Fake Internships

TARGETING STUDENTS

Scammers post internship ads on LinkedIn and Internshala with upfront payment demands and no real work.

Verify company registration at mca.gov.in
Cross-check on Glassdoor and their official site
Legitimate internships NEVER charge candidates money
📱

Telegram Scams

RAPIDLY GROWING — 2025

Fake recruiters use Telegram groups to conduct mass "interviews" and collect fees from hundreds of victims.

Real companies NEVER interview on Telegram
Block and report suspicious Telegram contacts immediately
Never join unsolicited recruitment channels
🪪

Identity Theft

LONG-TERM CONSEQUENCES

Scammers collect Aadhaar, PAN, and bank details under the guise of KYC for fake job offers.

Never share Aadhaar/PAN without verifying the recipient
Use masked Aadhaar from uidai.gov.in for online use
Report misuse to UIDAI helpline: 1947
💼

Job Fraud

MOST REPORTED TYPE

Fabricated listings on official-looking portals claiming top MNC roles. The position simply does not exist.

Only apply through official company career pages
Verify job IDs on the company's real website
Google "company + job fraud 2025" before applying
🕸️

Social Engineering

PSYCHOLOGICAL MANIPULATION

Manipulating victims through urgency, false authority, and emotional pressure to act without thinking.

Pause before acting on any "urgent" request
Verify independently via official phone numbers
Discuss with a trusted person before sharing anything
